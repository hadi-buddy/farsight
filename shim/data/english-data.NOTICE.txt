Data files used to recognise English words written in Persian letters
(generated by scripts/build-english.py):

en-sounds.txt
  Pronunciations: CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (cmudict), Carnegie Mellon
  University — BSD-style license.
  Word frequencies: wordfreq by Robyn Speer — data CC BY-SA 4.0.
  Dictionary flag / name filter: Webster's Second International (web2, public
  domain) via the `english-words` package (MIT).

en-pairs.txt
  English word pairs from symspellpy's frequency_bigramdictionary_en_243_342.txt
  (MIT).

fa-lexicon.txt
  Persian words with a part of speech from hazm's words.dat (MIT),
  https://github.com/roshan-research/hazm

fa-stopwords.txt
  Persian function words from hazm's stopwords.dat (MIT).
